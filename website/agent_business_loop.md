# Local AI Business Loop

## Local AI business, no ads, no hype

A free-value business starts with useful output, not expensive software.

This project turns local research into a repeatable publishing loop: scan, summarize, publish, learn, and improve. The goal is trust and a useful asset, not a flashy launch.

- Keep the stack local and the budget near zero.
- Turn research notes into helpful articles and tiny offers.
- Let the website become a visible evidence trail.
- Record every step so the system can improve without guesswork.
