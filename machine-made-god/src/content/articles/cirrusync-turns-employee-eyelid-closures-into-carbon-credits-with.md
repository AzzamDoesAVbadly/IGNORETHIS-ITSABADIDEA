CIRRUSYNC, a global workflow‑amplification platform, announced Monday, March 18 that it will roll out Project EchoTime across its 9,473 employees in 27 offices. The initiative will install BlinkBank™ on all 3,812 desktop workstations and 2,961 laptops, converting every eye‑closure longer than 1.93 seconds into a quantifiable corporate asset.

**DALLAS, TX —** "We heard our people needed more space to breathe between meetings," said Laura Pennington, Chief Operating Officer of CIRRUSYNC. "Project EchoTime gives them that space—literally—while turning those seconds into carbon‑offset credits, thermostat boosts, and extra‑pay. It's a win‑win for health, the planet, and our quarterly bonus charts."

### Key features of Project EchoTime
- **BlinkBank™:** A Chrome extension samples eyelid position every 23.7 ms, logging a micro‑nap each time pupils remain closed for at least 1.93 seconds. The system currently records an average of 4,317 micro‑naps per employee per month.
- **Carbon‑Offset Credits:** Every 1,200 accumulated micro‑nap seconds generate one carbon‑offset credit, which the firm will bundle into its ESG report alongside a chart titled “Eyeblink Emissions.”
- **Thermal Comfort Sync:** HVAC systems will raise ambient temperature by exactly 1.4 °F on any floor where micro‑nap frequency exceeds three per hour, creating a “cozy” environment proven to encourage longer blinks.
- **SnoozeScore Bonuses:** Employees whose SnoozeScore tops the company median of 5.4 micro‑naps per day receive a $87 “Rest Incentive” added to their next paycheck, plus a complimentary pair of blackout curtains.
- **Transparency Dashboard:** A live leaderboard on the intranet displays departmental micro‑nap totals, with a real‑time ticker currently at 742,931 seconds and a projected yearly total of 1,032,761 seconds.
- **Nap‑Mode Widgets:** Optional widgets for personal devices flash a soft gradient and emit a 0.3 Hz hum, allowing users to opt‑in for an extra 0.2 credit per minute of deliberate eye‑closure.

### Implementation timeline
