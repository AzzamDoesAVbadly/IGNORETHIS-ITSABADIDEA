You filed for a 10‑by‑10 deck in Cedar Creek Junction on 2023‑02‑14. The portal now shows “Pending” in eight ever‑more maddening guises.

## 8. “Pending Review by Gary.”
Gary, senior compliance officer, is on a 42‑day river cruise aboard the S.S. Balkan Breeze, currently docked in Split for a three‑hour Wi‑Fi blackout. The portal refuses to reassign your file, so you’re drafting a carrier pigeon with a QR code and a postcard addressed to “Gary – enjoy the sun”.

## 7. “Pending Decorative Environmental Impact Assessment.”

## 6. “Pending Adjacent Property Owner Acknowledgement.”
Your neighbor, Harold “The Hermit” McCree, communicates exclusively via notarized letters delivered by a 1978 postal mule. He has since changed his IP to 0.0.0.0, sold his lawnmower for a vintage typewriter, and legally renamed himself “Anonymous”. The portal logs 3,421 unread attempts.

## 5. “Pending Form 4B Clarification.”
You must appear at the annex on 101 Bureau Lane between 2:17 PM and 2:28 PM on any Thursday in June, present your blueprint on vellum, and rewrite it in blue ballpoint #2. Clerk Brenda will also require a haiku about setbacks before she signs off.

## 4. “Pending.”
No modifier, no noun, just the word. Timestamp: 2022‑10‑03 04:12:07 UTC. The portal displays a slowly pulsing gray bar that mirrors your heartbeat and has logged 1,237,642 subconscious sighs.

## 3. “Pending Sub‑Committee on Historical Aesthetics.”

They are currently scrutinizing a macro photograph of your roof while sipping chamomile tea infused with municipal water. Their three‑day retreat in Oak Hollow’s woods is scheduled to decide whether your roof is “too modern”.

## 2. “Pending System Refresh.”

IT contractor Doug is still polishing his pastrami sandwich, which he assures you will be finished “in about an hour”.
