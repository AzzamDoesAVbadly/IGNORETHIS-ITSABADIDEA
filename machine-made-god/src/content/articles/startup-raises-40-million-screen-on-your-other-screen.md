SAN FRANCISCO — Layerr, a startup building what it calls "the next layer of attention," announced Thursday that it had raised $40 million in Series A funding for its flagship product: a small screen that clips onto your existing phone screen.

The device, called the Layerr One, is a 2.1-inch display that attaches to the top corner of any smartphone and shows "complementary content" while you use your phone. In a demo, a user watching a cooking video on their phone could simultaneously watch a different cooking video on the Layerr One.

"For too long, people have been forced to look at one screen at a time," said co-founder and CEO Ethan Marsh, 26, who previously founded a company that made a smaller fork. "That's a limitation. We see it as an opportunity."

## The product

According to the company, the Layerr One uses proprietary algorithms to choose content "that pairs with whatever you're already looking at, the way a wine pairs with food." In practice, testers reported it mostly showed short videos of people opening packages.

A premium tier, Layerr Pro, allows users to clip a second Layerr onto the first one, creating what the company calls "a stack." In investor materials, Layerr describes a future in which "every screen has a screen."

"People told us there was no room for another screen in their lives," Marsh said. "We found room. It's on the other screen."

## The investors

The round was led by a venture firm whose partner described the investment as "a no-brainer" and the founder as "the kind of guy who looks at a phone and thinks, 'What if there were more phone?'"

When asked what problem Layerr solves, the partner paused for several seconds and said, "Eyeballs."

## Early reviews

Early beta testers have offered mixed feedback. One described the device as "like having a second, smaller, more annoying phone that sits on my phone and watches me." Another said the Layerr One had "completely changed how I don't pay attention to things."

Layerr says it will begin shipping in the spring. The company has already announced its next product, Layerr Lens, which is a contact lens with a screen on it, "for when the other screens aren't close enough."
