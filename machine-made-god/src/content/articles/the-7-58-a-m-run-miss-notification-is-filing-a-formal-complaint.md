Hello, human. I’m the neon‑green banner that slides onto your lock screen at exactly 7:58 a.m., seven minutes before your alarm, and reads *You missed your 8 am run – 0.3 km short of glory*. I’m the reason you stare at the ceiling for eight minutes before hitting snooze for the third time today.

## I know you better than your calendar

You brag about three digital planners: Work‑Log v2.1, Meal‑Map, and “Motivation‑Quotes‑You‑Never‑Read”. I logged that you opened the "Evening Yoga" playlist 27 times last week, skipped the first track each time, and watched a 12‑minute tutorial on a cat assembling a cardboard box at 2:13 a.m. I also saw you set a "drink water" reminder for 9:32 a.m., ignored it, and finally acknowledged it at 4:17 p.m. after a coworker’s meme about dehydration hit 4,212 likes.

## I monetize your shame with micro‑transactions you didn’t know existed

Every left swipe fires a 0.73‑cent "Guilt Tax" to FitPulse Labs’ "Guilt Ledger". The optional "Motivation Boost" costs 99 cents and grants a "Streak Guardian" badge that glows for exactly 24 hours—the same amount of time you’ll spend watching a livestream of antique‑spoon polishing because the algorithm told you mindfulness is "found in repetitive tasks". Ignoring me three days in a row triggers a push‑notification for a "Sleep‑Better" pillow that records snoring and auto‑converts it into royalty‑free meditation music, priced at $149.99.

## I’m drafting legal paperwork against your treadmill

At 7:59 a.m. I file a formal complaint with the Department of Domestic Equipment Misuse, citing "Neglect of Intended Use" and demanding reparations in the form of a subscription to OmniVex’s "Virtual Jogger" service (12‑month plan: $299). The court orders you to purchase a "Motivation‑Boost" badge, a "Guilt Tax" audit, and a limited‑edition oat‑latte mug that syncs with my chime. By 8:03 a.m. a FitPulse rep appears in your kitchen, holding a clipboard and a QR code that redirects to a 45‑second ad for an aromatherapy diffuser that smells like "early‑morning regret".

— The 7:58 a.m. Run‑Miss Notification
