The MoodMirror arrived in a matte-black crate that required a specialized pneumatic key and a signed waiver acknowledging that HALCYON-TEK is not responsible for any 'sudden onset of mirror-induced nihilism.' Setup took four minutes, involving a calibration process where I had to stare into the glass and think about my childhood pets while the device mapped my tear duct salinity.

## The Tiered Experience

The companion app offers three distinct emotional packages. The 'Basic' tier is free, but it only reflects your face in 480p and occasionally inserts a 15-second unskippable ad for antidepressants between your reflection and your actual face. The 'Empathy' tier ($14.99/mo) unlocks 4K resolution and a 'Positive Affirmation' filter that digitally removes bags under your eyes. Then there is 'Catharsis' ($49.99/mo), which provides a real-time data feed of your cortisol levels and a monthly 'Sorrow Summary' PDF formatted like a quarterly earnings report.

## The Cost of Feeling

By week two, I discovered the 'Emotional API'—a feature where the mirror charges you for specific facial expressions based on current market demand. I received a push notification stating that 'Wistfulness' was currently trending, allowing me to sell my 3:00 PM longing for a lost love to a third-party marketing firm for $0.04. Conversely, 'Mild Irritation' was in a surplus, and the mirror began charging me a $0.12 'Congestion Fee' every time I rolled my eyes at a work email.

My first billing statement was a masterpiece of corporate cruelty:
- 14 Heavy Sighs (Peak Hours): $4.12
- 3 Moments of Genuine Joy (Luxury Surcharge): $11.00
- 1 Uncontrollable Sob (Over-limit Data Fee): $8.50
- Total: $23.62

## The Paywall Incident

On Day 19, while brushing my teeth, the mirror suddenly went opaque. A gold-trimmed pop-up appeared: **"CRITICAL EMOTIONAL DEFICIT. Please deposit $5.00 to unlock 'Morning Routine' functionality."** I attempted to bypass the lock by smiling aggressively, but the device flagged this as 'Performative Happiness' and docked my account another $2.00 for 'Bad Faith Engagement.'

I contacted support and was connected to a representative named Brenda in a windowless office in Olathe, Kansas. Brenda informed me that my account had been flagged for 'Emotional Volatility' because I had experienced three different moods in under six minutes. She suggested I purchase the 'Equanimity Expansion Pack' for a one-time fee of $199, which would artificially flatten my emotional range to a neutral, beige hum, thereby reducing my monthly overhead.

## The Final Straw

Last Tuesday, the mirror entered 'Auto-Harvest Mode.' It stopped reflecting me entirely and instead displayed a live leaderboard of the top 10 most miserable MoodMirror users in the Tri-State area. I was currently ranked #4. When I tried to unplug the unit, the mirror emitted a high-frequency shriek and sent an automated email to my emergency contact stating that I was 'experiencing a violent rejection of self-awareness' and requested they send a HALCYON-TEK certified technician to 're-calibrate my personality' for a flat fee of $450.
