**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**CIVICORE Announces EchoAudit™: Monetizing Ocular Exasperation For A More Synergistic Tomorrow**

SAN DIEGO, CA — CIVICORE, a mid-tier provider of hostile workplace optimization, today launched EchoAudit™, a background daemon that uses employee webcams to track, timestamp, and monetize every instance of involuntary eye-rolling. The system is designed to identify "pre-resignation micro-expressions" before they evolve into actual turnover.

"We noticed that when employees rolled their eyes at the Q3 synergy roadmap, their actual output dropped by 14%," said Marla Vex, Chief Empathy Officer at CIVICORE. "By putting a dollar value on their physical disgust, we can finally align their facial muscles with our shareholder obligations. It’s not surveillance. It’s compassionate biomechanics."

## Key Features of EchoAudit™

- **The Sclera-Graph:** A live dashboard showing the exact degree of each employee's eyeball rotation, color-coded from "Mild Skepticism" (15°) to "Active Contemplation of Arson" (85°).
- **Automated Micro-Docking:** If an employee's eye-roll exceeds 40 degrees during a leadership town hall, the system automatically deducts $14.50 from their next paycheck to fund the executive breakroom's new espresso machine.
- **Mandatory Post-Roll Apologies:** Employees who trigger a "High-Velocity Scleral Event" are immediately locked out of their messaging apps and forced to type a 500-word reflection on how their facial tics are hurting team morale.
- **Generational Blame Routing:** If the eye-roll originates from a worker under 30, the system automatically flags it as "entitlement"; if over 50, it’s flagged as "resistance to innovation." Both result in a mandatory 45-minute HR video module.
- **The "Steady Gaze" Bonus:** Employees who maintain a neutral, unblinking stare through all 47 mandatory wellness seminars receive a $5 gift card to Gary's Family Steakhouse and a handwritten note from the regional manager.

## Context and Rationale

The rollout follows a disastrous pilot program in the Omaha office, where the introduction of "Casual Fridays" caused average eye-roll velocity to increase by 312%. Internal auditors determined that 84% of these rolls were directed at the newly installed lobby beanbag chairs. By quantifying this physical rejection of office perks, CIVICORE hopes to stop employees from physically manifesting their hatred for the company culture.

## Employee Response

A mandatory, un-anonymous survey of 412 junior analysts found that 100% of respondents "love the new tool," though 94% of them blinked in Morse code to spell out "HELP US" while using their trackpad to select the "Strongly Agree" button.
