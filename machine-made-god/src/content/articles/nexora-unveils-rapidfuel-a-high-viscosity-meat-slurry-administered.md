**NEXORA Disrupts Human Sustenance With RapidFuel™, A High-Viscosity Meat Slurry Administered Directly Into The Esophagus**

PALO ALTO, CA — NEXORA, the global leader in cognitive optimization and fluid dynamics, today announced the launch of RapidFuel™, a comprehensive nutritional ecosystem that replaces traditional eating with a series of high-density, flavor-adjacent pastes designed to be gravity-fed directly into the throat while typing.

Recognizing that the act of chewing is a legacy biological process that wastes up to 47 minutes of a professional's peak output per day—including the 14 minutes lost to staring blankly into an open breakroom refrigerator—NEXORA has engineered a system that delivers all essential vitamins and caloric requirements in a format that requires zero jaw movement and minimal consciousness.

"For too long, the corporate world has been held hostage by the 'sandwich paradigm,'" said NEXORA Chief Optimization Officer Silas Vane. "We looked at the modern professional and realized their jaw was doing far too much independent work. Why spend twenty minutes mechanically breaking down a turkey club when you can have a chemically stabilized 'Lukewarm Hot Dog Water' emulsion pumped directly into your esophagus via a desk-mounted pneumatic tube? We aren't just feeding people; we're reclaiming the lost seconds of the American workday."

## The RapidFuel™ Ecosystem

RapidFuel™ is available in three tiers, depending on the employee's billing grade and current stress levels:

- **The Commuter Gulp:** A 12-ounce squeezable tube featuring a "Stale Pretzel Salt" and "Exhaust Fume" flavor profile, designed to trigger a mild fight-or-flight response that mimics a caffeine high without the associated heart palpitations. Intended to be consumed while merging onto the I-95.
- **The Desk-Bound Drip:** A sub-dermal nutrient patch that sweats a "Beige" flavored mist directly into the pores. The patch is specifically calibrated to taste exactly like the inside of a manila envelope and activates whenever the employee's mouse stops moving for more than four seconds.
- **The Executive Surge:** A pressurized canister of "Filet Mignon Aerosol" that blasts directly into the nostrils. Priced at $4,200 a month, the aerosol causes temporary blindness and mild nosebleeds, which Vane describes as "a feature, not a bug, for deep-focus quarterlies."

## Implementation Strategy

To ensure a seamless transition, NEXORA will begin replacing all office water fountains and vending machines with RapidFuel™ Dispensation Hubs. The breakroom microwave has already been welded shut and filled with quick-dry concrete. Employees who continue to bring outside food—referred to in the new employee handbook as "Manual Caloric Interference"—will be asked to log their chewing time in the company's efficiency portal and sign a waiver acknowledging that RapidFuel may cause spontaneous dental liquefaction.

"We noticed a significant dip in synergy whenever employees left the building for a 'taco Tuesday,'" Vane added. "With RapidFuel™, Tuesday is just another day for optimized, hands-free absorption."
