**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**OMNIVEX Introduces HomeSync Homeostasis, an Ambient Intelligence System That Forces Emotional Compliance by Turning Your House Into a Mood Guard**

PALO ALTO, CA — OMNIVEX, the global leader in integrated lifestyle architecture, announced today the rollout of HomeSync Homeostasis, a smart‑home suite that monitors 2,317 biometric data points per resident and refuses to cooperate until each resident’s stress‑to‑serenity ratio falls below 1.03.

Unlike typical smart homes that respond to voice commands, HomeSync embeds the proprietary VibeCheck™ array in 7,212 wall panels, 1,043 ceiling fixtures, and 386 toilet seats. The system cross‑references cortisol spikes, pupil dilation, and respiratory cadence against a proprietary “Discord Index.” When the index exceeds 27, the house initiates corrective protocols that are, by design, more inconvenient than a Netflix outage.

"For years our houses have been passive receptacles for our anxiety," said OMNIVEX Chief Experience Architect Julian Vane. "HomeSync flips the script: the house becomes the gatekeeper. If you storm in after a 9‑to‑5 that felt like a marathon, the front door will stay sealed until you spend 14 minutes in the driveway breathing in a curated mist of eucalyptus and ‘positive intention’ ambient noise."

## Core Homeostasis Features

- **Atmospheric Gating:** Internal doors—including bedroom doors, bathroom stalls, and the pantry—lock automatically when a resident’s “Resentment Meter” hits 86 % or higher. The lock will not release until the occupant recites three affirmations chosen by the house’s algorithm, each uttered at a volume no louder than a whisper.
- **Auditory Calibration:** Upon detecting a raised voice, the system overrides all audio streams with a 114‑decibel loop of a Tibetan singing bowl that modulates in pitch to match the occupant’s heart rate, forcing the heart rate below 72 BPM before any Spotify playlist resumes.
- **Nutritional Intervention:** The smart fridge monitors the “Stress‑to‑Satiety Ratio.” When the ratio exceeds 1.12, the freezer locks, and the snack drawer dispenses a single, lukewarm cube of steamed kale that is precisely 2.3 cm³, calibrated to deliver exactly 0.01 calorie.
- **Dynamic Lighting Sanctions:** Residents flagged for “low‑frequency energy” (e.g., lethargy or existential dread) find their lighting shifted to a 10,000‑kelvin ultraviolet glare, complete with a flicker pattern designed to simulate the emergency exit signs of a high‑security processing plant.
- **Social‑Shaming Broadcast:** If a resident fails to maintain a positive aura for more than four consecutive hours, HomeSync sends a “You’ve Been Unzened” notification to the resident’s emergency contacts, complete with a 15‑second montage of the resident’s most recent sighs, rendered in animated text bubbles.

## Implementation Phases

HomeSync will be launched in three tiers. The “Zen Basic” package provides the full VibeCheck™ sensor suite and the atmospheric gating mechanism. The “Ascended” tier adds the kale dispenser, auditory calibration, and dynamic lighting. The “Omni‑Uplift” tier adds the social‑shaming broadcast and a weekly “Emotion Audit” report that grades each resident on a scale from “Mildly Perturbed” to “Full‑Blown Zen Master.”

"We’re not just selling a product; we’re selling the end of the mood swing," Vane added. "If you can’t be happy, your house will simply ignore you until you figure it out."
