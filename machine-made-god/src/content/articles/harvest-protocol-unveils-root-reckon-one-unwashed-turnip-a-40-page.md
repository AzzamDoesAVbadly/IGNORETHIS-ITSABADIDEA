**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**HARVEST PROTOCOL Launches “Root & Reckon,” a Meal‑Kit Tier Consisting of One Unwashed Turnip and a 40‑Page Essay on Soil Acidity**

BURLINGTON, VT — Harvest Protocol, the direct‑to‑consumer botanical disruption startup that calls its products “time‑capsules,” announced today that its new “Minimalist Tier” will eliminate the most time‑intensive part of dinner: the act of actually eating.

The kit contains exactly one (1) medium‑sized turnip weighing 2.73 lb, harvested at 03:17 AM on 2026‑09‑12 from the “Sullen Ridge” farm in southern Vermont. The turnip arrives still clinging to a 7 cm × 4 cm patch of damp topsoil, sealed in a biodegradable cellophane sleeve printed with the phrase “I’m rooting for you.”

Alongside the root is a 40‑page, 12,000‑word, peer‑reviewed essay titled *Historical pH Levels of Loam Basin 4*, printed on 100 % recycled hemp paper in 4‑point Garamond. The essay includes 23 footnotes referencing the “Ancient Turnip Council” and a bibliography of 19 obscure soil‑science journals.

> “Our users were spending up to 45 minutes a day cooking, chewing, and digesting,” said Harvest Protocol Chief Culinary Officer Aris Thorne, who also happens to be a certified meditation guide. “We asked ourselves: what if dinner were just a concept you could hold in one hand? Root & Reckon turns that idea into a tactile experience without the friction of nutrition.”

## Key features (escalating)

- **The Turnip:** Grown on a single plot that receives exactly 4 hours of sunrise, 2 hours of cloud cover, and a weekly drizzle measured at 0.3 mm. Peeling, slicing, or cooking is strictly prohibited; users must cradle the turnip during video calls to signal “deep, root‑level work phase.”
- **The Essay:** Typeset in 4‑point font with a line spacing of 0.9, guaranteeing a minimum reading time of 3 hours 12 minutes. Margins are set to 0.13 inches to maximize paper usage and induce mild eye strain. Each page is stamped with a QR code linking to a live satellite feed that tracks the turnip’s eventual decomposition.
- **Asymmetric Hydration:** One (1) 8.2‑oz glass of tap water must be held at a precise 47° angle while the user contemplates the turnip’s existential journey. Water temperature must be 12 °C ±0.2 °C, measured with a calibrated kitchen thermometer.
- **Satellite Composting:** After contemplation, the turnip must be buried in a designated municipal green space (coordinates 42.8145° N, 73.2451° W). An accompanying NFC tag streams a real‑time carbon‑sequestration graph to the user’s phone.
- **Turnip Whisperer Hotline:** At exactly 02:13 AM each night, a retired agronomist named Milo Grange answers calls on a dedicated line (1‑800‑ROOT‑WHIS). He reads the essay aloud in a monotone voice while gently tapping the turnip with a wooden spoon.

## Context

The launch follows the recall of Harvest Protocol’s previous tier, “Just The Broth,” after 1,024 customers attempted to drink the broth and filed complaints that the vegetables refused to dissolve.

> “People were still experiencing hunger,” Thorne noted. “We realized hunger is a legacy mindset. Root & Reckon feeds the narrative, not the body.”

## Consumer response

Beta testers report feeling “lighter,” “more grounded,” and “confused about the proper way to dispose of soil.” Three therapy‑app partners have already added a “Post‑Turnip Counseling” module that includes guided breathing exercises and a 5‑minute visualization of the turnip’s decay.
