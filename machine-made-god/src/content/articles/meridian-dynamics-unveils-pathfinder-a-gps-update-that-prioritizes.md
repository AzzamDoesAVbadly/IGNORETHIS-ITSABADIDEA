**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**MERIDIAN DYNAMICS Optimizes the Commute with PathFinder: The First Navigation System That Routes Based Exclusively On Proximity To Middle Management**

**SPRINGFIELD, IL** — MERIDIAN DYNAMICS, a logistics and workforce alignment firm, today announced the rollout of PathFinder, an update to its proprietary fleet navigation software. PathFinder abandons the outdated metric of 'fastest route,' replacing it with a dynamic algorithm that prioritizes 'Synergy-Dense Corridors.'

Starting Tuesday, all 14,000 company-issued mid-size sedans and employee-synced smartphones will no longer calculate routes based on distance or traffic. Instead, PathFinder will redirect users toward high-value corporate clusters, partner vendor offices, and regional MERIDIAN DYNAMICS hubs, regardless of the user's intended destination.

"For too long, we have allowed the tyranny of the shortest distance to rob our employees of serendipitous professional encounters," said Julian Vane, Senior VP of Spatial Synergy at MERIDIAN DYNAMICS. "Why take the interstate to get home in 22 minutes when you could idle behind a freightliner outside the Oak Creek paper supply depot, just in case Greg from Accounting is grabbing a sandwich? That is where the real growth happens."

## Key Features of PathFinder

- **Networking Detours:** If the GPS detects that three or more of your direct reports are currently at a mid-tier soup and sandwich franchise within a 4-mile radius, it will automatically divert your route through the drive-thru, regardless of your dietary restrictions or stated destination.
- **The Synergy Loop:** If your daily 'Serendipity Score' falls below 400, the vehicle will lock the doors and enter 'Loop Mode,' circling the corporate campus at 14 mph until you make unbroken eye contact with a Vice President.
- **Hierarchy-Based Priority:** Executives are routed via 'Frictionless Arterials,' while Level 2 Associates are forced through 'Exposure Paths'—deliberately congested loading docks where they must physically witness the regional director’s new luxury sedan being hand-washed.
- **Biometric Vibe-Check:** Using integrated steering wheel galvanic skin response, PathFinder detects when an employee's heart rate drops below 'optimal quarterly anxiety' and immediately reroutes them to the loading dock of a regional paper supplier so you can watch a forklift drop a pallet of copy paper, reminding you of the fragility of your own deliverables.

## Implementation

PathFinder is currently being tested in the greater tri-county area. Early results show that while average commute times have increased by 214%, employee 'visibility' has reached an all-time high. 

"I just wanted to pick up my dry cleaning," says test participant Brenda Hessel, 41. "But the app detected that the VP of Logistics was getting a car wash at the adjacent strip mall, so it locked my steering wheel and drove me in a figure-eight pattern through the soapy runoff for forty-five minutes. I haven't seen my husband since Thursday, but my internal visibility score is in the 99th percentile."
