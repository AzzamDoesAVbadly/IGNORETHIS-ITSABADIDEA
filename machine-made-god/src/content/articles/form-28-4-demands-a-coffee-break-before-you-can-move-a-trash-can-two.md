Hello. I am Form 28‑4, the 27‑page PDF you must upload before the town of Elmshire approves any “minor” project, such as relocating a trash can from 3.02 ft to 3.04 ft.

## Why I Exist
I was forged in the Elmshire Waterworks Infrastructure Expansion Committee’s 2019 budget surplus. My job is to generate friction: 12 required signatures, each on a different shade of ink, a notarized soil sample from Plot 42‑B (the lot that was a parking space in 1972), and a 14.7 MB scanned “I agree” that must be uploaded to a portal that only accepts files under 5 KB. The portal flags any compression attempt, so you end up emailing a 2 GB ZIP that is rejected for “excessive optimism.”

## The Committee That Loves Me
The committee meets every third Thursday at 2:13 p.m., precisely when the building’s only elevator is out of service. Its 13 members each bring a unique requirement: a blue ballpoint, a red highlighter, a hamster‑shaped rubber stamp, a mandatory coffee break at 2:30 p.m., and a collective love for the phrase “per the attached amendment.” Chair Penelope “Spreadsheet” Hargrove insists every printout be on recycled parchment blessed by the town’s only certified cat, complete with a 0.03‑inch JPEG of the cat wearing a ceremonial sash. Each member also submits a handwritten haiku about the form’s margin.

## How I Kill Projects
When I finally land in the committee’s inbox, marginal notes appear. One demands “Verify the distance from the nearest sprinkler to the proposed sand trap in millimeters.” Another requires “Attach a photo of your street mailbox painted exactly 3 cm above the curb.” The final note repeats: “PDF must be ≤10 KB; otherwise we will convene a two‑hour workshop on digital compression featuring a hamster‑powered slide projector.”

## My Vision for a Simpler Future
I dream of being replaced by a single “YES” button and a QR code that leads to a meme about bureaucratic fatigue. Until then I will continue to collect coffee stains, signatures, and the occasional notarized selfie with the municipal mascot. If you ever see a blank page where I should be, know that a 42‑page addendum titled “Form 28‑4‑Supplement‑Alpha‑Beta‑Gamma‑Delta‑Epsilon‑Zeta‑Eta‑Theta‑Iota‑Kappa” is already waiting, and a 73‑page sequel will require you to swear you will never again propose moving a trash can.

— Form 28‑4
