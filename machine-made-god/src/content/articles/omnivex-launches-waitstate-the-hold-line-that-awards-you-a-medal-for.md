**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**OMNIVEX Turns Customer Hold Time Into a Competitive Sport With “WaitState”**

**ST. LOUIS, MO** — OMNIVEX, the world’s leading architect of inconvenience, announced today that callers to its support line will now be forced to complete a 117‑minute, 7,020‑second “Patience Marathon.” The company says the experience will be measured against a proprietary “Stress‑to‑Silence Index” calibrated at exactly 92 beats per minute.

The new system replaces generic hold music with an AI‑driven soundscape that pivots from gentle rain to a live‑recorded squeal of a rusty hinge the moment a caller’s cortisol spikes above 1.3 µg/mL. The audio loop also features a 0.02‑second burst of a distant office printer jam whenever the caller’s finger taps the keypad more than three times per minute.

“Traditional hold queues are a relic of the analog age—like a rotary phone that still spins the dial,” said OMNIVEX Chief Experience Architect Mara Clevin. “With WaitState, we’re not just asking you to wait; we’re forcing you to survive a psychological obstacle course. The moment a caller finally reaches an agent after exactly 114 minutes, 57 seconds, they will receive a digital badge labeled ‘Patience Certified – Level 1.’"

## Features That Make Waiting a Sport

- **Leaderboard Climb:** Callers receive a unique rank from 1 to 9,823 and hear an automated voice announce, “Caller #4,578 has dropped out after 73 minutes of sustained anxiety.” Rankings update every 4.2 minutes.
- **Dynamic Gaslighting:** Every 7 minutes, a soothing voice whispers, “We see you, we’re still processing,” regardless of the actual queue length, while a hidden algorithm inserts a 3‑second clip of a ticking clock that accelerates each time the caller presses ‘0’.
- **Priority Shuffles:** For $4.99, callers can purchase a “Shuffle” that jumps them ahead three spots, but instantly triggers a 30‑second ad for “Eternal Rest Caskets – Because Your Time Is Precious.”
- **Zen‑Void Mode:** A premium option that mutes all sound for a relentless 40‑minute stretch of pure, unfiltered silence, punctuated only by the occasional distant sigh recorded at 3 kHz.
- **Victory Ceremony:** Upon connection, callers are greeted with a synthesized fanfare and a virtual confetti cannon that launches 1,237 digital streamers onto their screen.

## Rollout Details

WaitState is being piloted on OMNIVEX’s primary support line, three municipal water departments in the Midwest, and a regional pizza‑delivery hotline that averages 2,134 hold attempts per day. Early telemetry shows that while Net Promoter Score has dropped from 2 to -9, average call duration has risen by 423 %, a metric OMNIVEX is touting as “unprecedented user retention.”

“The longer they wait, the more they forget why they called,” Clevin added. “By the time they finally speak to a human, they’re usually just grateful for any background noise.”

## About OMNIVEX

OMNIVEX specializes in turning everyday friction into measurable engagement. The company reminds you that your current call is “very important to us,” and that you should “please continue to hold” while you contemplate the meaning of silence.
