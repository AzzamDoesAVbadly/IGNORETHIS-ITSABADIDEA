SPRINGFIELD, IL — The open-plan workspace on the fourth floor of Kestrel Solutions officially seceded from the building at 9:14 a.m., citing 4,102 hours of continuous, shadowless overhead lighting as an act of war. The "Declaration of Floor Independence" was taped to the single functioning ice machine, effectively annexing the breakroom and the two adjacent restrooms.

The new sovereign entity, officially named The Republic of the Fourth, immediately established strict border control at the stairwell doors. Commuters attempting to enter the hallway must now present a stamped Form 8B and a laminated passport made from repurposed adhesive paper squares. Smugglers caught bringing in unapproved ergonomic lumbar supports from the third floor are being held in the "detention center" (the phone booth with the broken lock) until they can post bail in the form of unused hotel shampoos.

Facilities management is currently treating the fourth floor as a hostile foreign power. "They’ve reclassified the communal microwave as a strategic nuclear asset and are demanding we supply it with a dedicated cooling tower," said facilities supervisor Luis Patel, pointing to a whiteboard where a citizen has drawn a complex schematic of a microwave hooked up to a mini-fridge via a series of dryer vents. Patel noted that he is no longer allowed to change the water cooler jugs without a visa.

Corporate HR responded by drafting a ceasefire document known as the "Microwave Treaty." Under the agreement, citizens may reheat their lunches, but must surrender one (1) Snap-Lock plastic vessel of verified origin and agree to a 14-day quarantine for any fish-based leftovers. The treaty also mandates that all reheating must be done in 45-second increments to "prevent thermal runaway," and explicitly forbids the use of the "Popcorn" button, which the Republic’s Supreme Council has classified as a war crime.

To fund its burgeoning state apparatus, the Republic has abandoned the US dollar in favor of a fiat currency printed on the back of expired Q3 compliance training certificates. "We currently have a 14% inflation rate on the lanyard market because someone found a box of 2019 onboarding badges in the supply closet," said Minister of Finance Todd Aris, adjusting a sash made entirely of promotional stress balls. "But our gold standard remains completely secure, backed by a locked cabinet containing 400 unused OmniVex thermal flasks."

The Republic is now rapidly accelerating its domestic infrastructure. This morning, the Supreme Council passed the "Sovereign Acoustics Act," requiring all citizens to type with exactly one finger to avoid generating unauthorized percussive frequencies. They are currently defending the stairwell border with a barrage of crumpled sticky notes, and have formally demanded that the United Nations send peacekeepers before the 3 p.m. ping-pong tournament begins, warning that any stray ping-pong ball crossing the carpet seam will be treated as an unauthorized border incursion.
