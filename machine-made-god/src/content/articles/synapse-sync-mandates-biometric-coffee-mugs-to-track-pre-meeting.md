**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**SYNAPSE SYNC Announces Project MugSense: Real-Time Emotion Analytics via Company-Issued Coffee Vessels**

CHAPEL HILL, NC — SYNAPSE SYNC today retrofitted 8,432 employee coffee mugs with micro-photodiodes, pressure transducers, and a proprietary "Mug Mood Engine." Beginning Monday, the enterprise-grade ceramics will translate minute facial micro-expressions directly into the Q3 performance dashboard.

"We realized the 10:15 AM coffee run was a massive black box of unmonetized emotional data," said Lila Moritz, Chief Hydration Officer. "Now, when you stare blankly into your medium roast, the MugSense engine captures that exact frequency of existential dread and pipes it straight to your manager's wearable tracker."

## Key Features & Next Steps

- **Live Sip-Score Ambient Lighting:** The mug’s handle glows green for optimal morale, yellow for mild fatigue, and pulses a deep, urgent crimson if the user thinks about quitting before 11:00 AM.
- **Tear-Salinity Calibration:** If the MugSense detects a saline spike above 0.9%—indicating a single tear has fallen into the Earl Grey—it automatically pauses all outgoing direct messages and deploys a digital "Hang In There" kitten to the user's desktop.
- **Grief-to-Sprint Conversion:** Should an employee experience a personal tragedy, the mug’s thermal sensors detect the resulting drop in core hand temperature and automatically convert their bereavement leave into 40 hours of unmapped data-entry sprints.
- **Subvocalization Lip-Reading:** The rim features microscopic vibration sensors that read the silent, angry words employees mouth while waiting for the breakroom coffee urn, translating "I hate this place" into a formal Performance Improvement Plan draft.
- **Post-Mortem Ceramic Recycling:** Upon an employee's termination or death, the mug is immediately kiln-fired into a commemorative paperweight for the lobby, ensuring their final sighs remain structurally load-bearing.

## Context & Rationale

During a six-month pilot at the Chapel Hill campus, the MugSense engine successfully identified 14 instances of quiet quitting, three impending resignations, and one employee whose resting face naturally registered as "severe clinical burnout," resulting in their immediate promotion to Regional Director.

## Employee Reaction

Internal polling shows 94% of staff feel "optimistically surveilled." The remaining 6% who attempted to organize a walkout were automatically issued replacement mugs that only dispense lukewarm tap water and play a continuous, 40-decibel recording of a dial-up modem.

## About SYNAPSE SYNC

SYNAPSE SYNC is a leading provider of workplace-first data pipelines. The company is headquartered in a windowless brutalist structure in Chapel Hill and is currently beta-testing a smart-toilet attachment that measures flush-velocity to predict Q4 retention rates.
