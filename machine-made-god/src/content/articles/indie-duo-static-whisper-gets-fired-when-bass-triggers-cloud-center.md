## An unexpected power draw

The opening track, “Latency Love,” featured an eight‑second riff tuned to 27.3 Hz, the exact frequency Nexora’s fire‑suppression AI flags as a potential thermal anomaly. The engineers responded with a chorus of polite applause punctuated by frantic typing.
