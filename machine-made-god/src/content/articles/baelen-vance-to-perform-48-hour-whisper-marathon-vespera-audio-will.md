OAKHAVEN, OH — Vespera Audio today confirmed that platinum-recording artist Baelen Vance will headline the “Hush-Hush World Tour,” a 48-hour continuous livestream where he whispers the entirety of his 14-track album *Tethered to the Drywall*. To ensure uncompromised acoustic intimacy, Vespera will push a mandatory over-the-air firmware update to all 42 million registered users at 4:00 AM Tuesday, permanently disabling device speakers, headphone jacks, and wireless audio pairing for the duration of the broadcast.

Vance, 26, has spent the last eight months preparing his vocal cords for the marathon by consuming only room-temperature distilled water and inhaling the steam of boiled celery. “A normal whisper is 30 decibels. Baelen is pushing 14 decibels, which is the exact acoustic footprint of a dust mite digesting a skin flake,” said Vespera Chief Acoustic Officer Aris Thorne. “We had to invent a new microphone just to pick it up. It’s essentially a seismograph glued to a stethoscope.”

## Key features of the Hush-Hush Experience

- **Mandatory Decibel Monitoring**: The firmware patch repurposes your device’s microphone to monitor your living room. If your ambient noise exceeds 22 dB—including breathing heavily, petting a dog, or the refrigerator compressor kicking on—your stream will instantly replace Baelen’s face with a flashing red QR code that charges your linked credit card a $14.99 Ambient Noise Fine.
- **Haptic Apologies**: If the system registers a cough or a sneeze from the viewer, the phone’s vibration motor will activate at maximum intensity for 14 seconds to simulate the physical sensation of letting down a deeply disappointed parent.
- **Premium Silence Tiers**: While the base stream is free, “Platinum Hush” subscribers ($89.99/month) receive a live, real-time heat map showing exactly which of their friends in a 50-mile radius are currently failing to remain quiet.
- **Biometric Vocal Cord Paralysis**: Live attendees at the three physical “Whisper Domes” in Ohio, Nevada, and Luxembourg must sign a waiver allowing Vespera medics to administer a localized, temporary laryngeal paralytic via a dart gun to prevent accidental throat-clearing.

The Hush-Hush World Tour follows Vance’s 2023 “Sensory Deprivation Residency” in a decommissioned grain silo, which was cut short after 11 hours when a fan accidentally exhaled too loudly, triggering the venue’s automated fire suppression system and flooding the stage with 4,000 gallons of halon gas. Vespera executives insist the new firmware will prevent a repeat of the Silo Incident, noting that halon gas is entirely incompatible with modern acoustic dampening foam.

When asked about the logistical challenges of broadcasting a 14-decibel audio stream over standard cellular networks, Thorne stated that Vespera has simply lowered the legal definition of sound in their updated terms of service. “If the data packet arrives, it’s audio,” Thorne explained, before whispering his own follow-up question to a subordinate so as not to disturb the celery steam. Vance is already planning his next project, a 72-hour mime residency where the audience must legally forget the concept of physical space.
