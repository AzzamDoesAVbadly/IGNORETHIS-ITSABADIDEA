BAYSFIELD, CA — Enterprise software giant NEXORA announced Tuesday the immediate sunsetting of all departmental chat divisions, collapsing its workforce of 18,400 employees into a single, unmoderated direct message channel titled `#general-trench-all-hands-live`.

The initiative, code-named Project Clean Slate, aims to eliminate “siloed friction” by ensuring that corporate restructuring announcements, facility gas leak notices, and animated GIFs of raccoon behavior appear in the exact order they are generated by human hands or automated Jira ticketing bots.

"Under the previous architecture, an employee in Accounts Receivable could spend up to four minutes looking for the correct channel to report a payroll discrepancy," said Senior Director of Frictionless Throughput Greg Hallowell, speaking over the dull hum of 400 laptop cooling fans. "Now, that inquiry is delivered instantly into the same central stream where Gary from Logistics is explaining his personal recipe for pickled beets to the entire Board of Directors."

According to internal documentation, the Trench processes an average of 4,110 messages per second. Custom emojis have been disabled and replaced with two non-negotiable reactions: an icon of a gray concrete pylon, and an auto-populating button that inserts the phrase "Understood, circling back at cadence."

Early operational reviews indicate the merge has flattened company hierarchy overnight:

* At 9:14 AM, Chief Financial Officer Marsha Sterling’s announcement regarding mandatory unpaid furloughs was pushed out of readable screen space within three-tenths of a second by twenty-six automated alerts about a malfunctioning label printer on the dock in Gary, Indiana.
* At 11:32 AM, a junior copywriter asking if anyone had spare ibuprofen received an immediate thumbs-up reaction from the automated severance bot.
* At 1:04 PM, a three-way thread between Legal, Compliance, and a mid-level QA tester who accidentally copy-pasted his mother’s grocery list was declared an official corporate milestone by the company's automated recognition tracker, awarding all participants 40 NEXORA Merit Tokens.

"I tried to notify facilities that the third-floor vending machine was smoking, but my message was immediately bracketed by four hundred employees typing 'Happy Monday team!' and a automated reminder to hydrate," said Ingestion Associate Maya Chen, 26, whose monitor currently renders a solid gray blur of high-velocity text. "I just stared at the stream until my vision blurred into a gray smear. Then I reacted with the pylon. I think I got promoted."

NEXORA confirmed that all future direct messaging between individuals has been permanently deprecated to prevent "rogue offline whispering." Employees attempting to step away from the Trench for more than ninety seconds will have their status automatically changed to "Hesitating," alerting the entire stream with an audible chime.
