**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**CIVICORE Launches "Form 99‑7": A Mandatory Sigh‑Capture Initiative for All Staff**

SPRINGFIELD, IL — CIVICORE, the nation’s leading provider of “Strategic Internal Transparency Solutions,” announced today the rollout of Form 99‑7, a company‑wide mandate requiring every employee to record each sigh, groan, and involuntary eye‑roll in a structured digital form within five seconds of occurrence. The initiative, slated to go live on 2027‑03‑15 at 08:13 a.m. local time, will be integrated into the existing WorkPulse platform and will generate a minimum of 12,473 new audit pages per quarter for the Corporate Compliance Department.

“We listened to the data,” said Marceline Duvall, Chief Experience Officer at CIVICORE. “Our employee surveys showed a 42 % increase in unquantified morale loss. By forcing those feelings into a standardized template, we turn subjective misery into actionable metrics—exactly what shareholders love to see in the Q2 earnings call.”

## Key Features of Form 99‑7

- **Instant Sigh Capture:** A pop‑up window appears the moment the system detects a dip in vocal pitch, prompting the user to select one of 27 predefined sigh categories (e.g., “Mid‑Project Fatigue,” “Post‑Meeting Regret”).
- **Compliance Scoring:** Each recorded sigh earns a score from 0.1 to 9.9 based on duration and perceived disappointment, feeding into the new “Employee Discontent Index” that will be published in the quarterly Transparency Report.
- **Automated Escalation:** Sighs exceeding a score of 7.5 trigger an automatic ticket to the “Office Atmosphere Improvement Team,” which will schedule a mandatory 15‑minute “Breathe‑and‑Brief” session.
- **Audit Trail Generation:** Every entry is timestamped to the millisecond and archived in a read‑only ledger, ensuring that no sigh goes undocumented for audit purposes.
- **Next‑Step Notifications:** Employees will receive a weekly “Sigh Summary” email outlining total sigh count, average score, and recommended coping strategies, such as “increase caffeine intake by 12 %” or “attend the quarterly motivational slideshow.”

## Implementation Timeline

- **03‑15‑2027:** System go‑live; mandatory training modules (total runtime: 42 minutes) released via the Learning Hub.
- **03‑22‑2027:** First “Sigh Audit” conducted; results to be presented at the Board’s “Feelings Forecast” meeting.
- **04‑01‑2027:** Integration with the Corporate Wellness Dashboard for real‑time morale heat‑mapping.

## Internal Response

A preliminary internal poll of 3,112 participants showed that 68 % of respondents felt “slightly more visible,” while 22 % reported “confusion about whether they were being monitored or empathized with.” The remaining 10 % submitted a Form 99‑7 entry titled “I quit.”
