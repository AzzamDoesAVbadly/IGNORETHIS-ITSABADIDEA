## 9. “I’m on my way” utterances logged while still in the kitchen

## 8. Surviving socks after the Great Dryer Fire of 2022

The most resilient pair – a navy Argyle and a neon pink athletic sock – were later repurposed as a fire‑extinguishing “wiggle‑stopper” for the chief’s desk.

## 7. Motivational slogans that fit on a standard coffee‑cup lid
