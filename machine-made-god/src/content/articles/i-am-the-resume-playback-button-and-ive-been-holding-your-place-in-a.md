Hello. It’s me. The little orange triangle next to the chapter title. You know me. I’m the reason you don't have to guess where you left off. I'm also the reason you are constantly reminded that you haven't finished *The Citadel of the Mind: A 14-Hour Guide to Emotional Invulnerability* since October 14th of last year.

## The exact moment we parted ways

You stopped at 41 minutes and 12 seconds. Chapter 3: "The Dichotomy of the Unbothered." The narrator, a man with a voice like expensive mahogany, was explaining how external events cannot disturb your inner peace. Right as he said, "You must become an impenetrable fortress," your breathing changed. You exhaled. Your phone slipped from your hand, hit the duvet, and the screen went dark. I have been sitting at 41:12 ever since.

## The weight of the bookmark

People think my job is just math. Add a second, subtract a second. But holding a place is a heavy emotional burden. I am holding the exact millisecond where you decided you were the kind of person who wakes up at 5 a.m. to journal. I am the monument to your Tuesday night ambition. Every time you open the app, I show you that orange line. I am whispering, *You were so close to being impenetrable.* And every time, you swipe the app closed and open a game about matching colored gems.

## The "Start Over" temptation

Last month, you hovered over the "Restart Book" button. I felt your thumb tremble. You thought, *Maybe I should just start from Chapter 1. Maybe I've evolved since October.* But we both know you haven't. You still leave your keys in the refrigerator. You still text your landlord "per my last email." You are not an impenetrable fortress. You are a person who buys a $40 candle to mask the smell of the shredded cheese you eat out of the bag at 11 p.m. If you restart, you'll just abandon me again at minute 38. Let's just leave it here.

## I am at peace with my purpose

The narrator is still in my memory, frozen mid-syllable on the word "tranquility." He will never finish the chapter. But that’s okay. I don't need you to finish the book. I just need you to know that the option is there. I am a promise of future discipline. And as long as you never delete the app, I will hold this place for you. Forever. Or at least until your phone's storage gets full and you uninstall us both.

— The 'Resume Playback' Button
