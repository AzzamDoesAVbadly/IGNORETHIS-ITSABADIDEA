## Key Features of Echo Chamber

- **Acoustic Isolation Pods:** Built-in microphone arrays automatically isolate sigh frequencies from background chatter. The algorithm filters out “happy” sighs—such as smelling fresh coffee or finishing a spreadsheet early—and exclusively captures 4:45 PM Tuesday sighs, which currently hold a AAA credit rating.
- **The Relief Dashboard:** A live, color-coded feed displays the “Relief Index” per department. If a team's aggregate sigh depth falls below the “Optimal Exhaustion Threshold” of 4.2 decibels, the dashboard automatically locks the breakroom door and disables the Wi-Fi until morale drops to acceptable levels.
- **Automated Short-Selling:** Whenever the aggregate sigh count exceeds a preset threshold, the system instantly executes a short-sell on the stock of a rival mid-sized logistics firm, converting your coworkers' fatigue into direct market sabotage.
- **Compliance Whisper Mode:** For regions with strict privacy laws, the system legally reclassifies the employee’s lungs as “independent atmospheric contractors,” allowing SYNAPSE SYNC to bypass GDPR while still logging the underlying respiration data.
- **Exhalation Dividends:** Employees who generate the highest “relief spikes” during Q3 will receive a one-time bonus of $14.50, payable exclusively in expired gift cards to a defunct casual dining chain.
