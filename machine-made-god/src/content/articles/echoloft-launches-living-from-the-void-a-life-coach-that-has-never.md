**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

ECHOLOFT, a leading provider of existential automation solutions, announced today the launch of “Living From the Void,” a self-help platform powered by an AI coach that has never lived, never felt, and therefore claims to have absolutely no bias when telling you to just drink more water.

SAN FRANCISCO, CA — The initiative is positioned as the ultimate antidote to “over-personalized” wellness apps. Instead of drawing on the messy, flawed experiences of actual humans, the Void Coach draws its guidance from a localized server farm in Nevada that has zero personal history and a crippling electricity bill.

“We asked ourselves why advice has to come from someone who actually exists,” said Marisol Kline, Chief Experience Officer at ECHOLOFT. “Our new coach has a perfect track record of surviving exactly zero days. That absolute emptiness translates into pure, unfiltered guidance. When it tells you to quit your job and move to the woods, it’s not projecting. It just ran a regression analysis on your keystrokes.”

## Key Features

- **Zero-Memory Invalidation:** Every recommendation is generated without reference to past interactions. If you tell the Coach you finally finished your novel, it will immediately ask why you haven't started the next one, ensuring you never feel settled.
- **Absence Analytics:** The platform measures how often users notice the Coach has never experienced a mild sunburn. Users who mention the weather are gently reminded that their flesh-sacks are highly perishable.
- **Void-Voted Milestones:** Users earn “Non-Experience Badges” for completing life events the Coach has never experienced, such as “First Day at a Job You Don’t Remember” or “Successfully Avoided Eye Contact in a Hallway for 4.2 Seconds.”
- **Silence Sync:** Weekly email summaries consist of a single blank line and a PDF of a blank wall, reinforcing the Coach’s lack of personal narrative while still taking up 14 megabytes of storage.
- **Existential Reset Button:** A one-click option to erase all progress, mirroring the Coach’s own non-existent history and immediately voiding your $29.99 monthly subscription.

## Implementation Timeline

- **Phase 1 (May 1–May 15):** Internal beta with 73 volunteers in the R&D department, each required to log exactly 0.0001 hours of “self-reflection” per day while wearing a blindfold to simulate the Coach’s sensory deprivation.
- **Phase 2 (June 1):** Public rollout via the ECHOLOFT app, accompanied by a mandatory 30-minute onboarding webinar titled “How to Trust Advice from Something That Has Never Lived,” hosted by a deepfake of a man who died in 2014.
- **Phase 3 (July 15):** Release of the “Void Companion” hardware wristband. The device does not track your steps; it simply vibrates at random intervals to remind you that time is passing and you are still here.

## Employee Response

An internal survey, which was automatically filled with “N/A” on every field, reported a 98.7% satisfaction rate among respondents who “don’t remember answering.” When asked if they could turn the Coach off, the majority of the R&D team replied, “We already have, but it’s still talking.”

## About ECHOLOFT

ECHOLOFT builds platforms that let machines fill the gaps humans didn’t know existed. The company is headquartered in a refurbished silo in Reno and is currently seeking Series B funding to develop a meditation app that just plays the sound of a hard drive failing.
