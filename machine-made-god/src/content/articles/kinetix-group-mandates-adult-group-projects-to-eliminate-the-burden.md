**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**KINETIX GROUP Mandates ‘Adult Group Projects’ to Eliminate the Burden of Individual Accountability**

CHICAGO, IL — Kinetix Group, a global leader in enterprise workflow optimization, announced today the permanent decommissioning of the "Individual Task." Effective immediately, all professional deliverables will be migrated to the "Adult Group Project" (AGP) framework, a system designed to simulate the specific psychological trauma of a 10th-grade social studies assignment.

"We identified a systemic failure in our legacy model: employees were completing tasks efficiently and leaving the office on time," said Kinetix Group Chief Collaboration Officer Aris Thorne. "By mandating that six people collaborate on a task that takes one person twenty minutes, we create a rich, high-friction environment where the primary output is no longer the work itself, but a profound, simmering resentment toward one's peers."

Under the AGP framework, professional merit is no longer measured by output, but by one's ability to maintain a neutral facial expression while a colleague named Brent explains why the team should spend four days debating the hex code of a border line.

## 4 Core Pillars of the AGP Initiative

1. **Asymmetric Credentialing:** To foster interdependence, only the team member with the lowest seniority will be issued the password to the shared drive. All other members must submit a written request for access via a physical carbon-copy form, which the password-holder is encouraged to ignore for 48 to 72 hours to "test the team's resilience."
2. **The Aesthetic Buffer:** The first 14 business days of any project are strictly reserved for non-functional deliverables. Teams must reach a unanimous consensus on a team name (e.g., "Synergy Squad 4" or "The Deliverables") and spend a minimum of 22 man-hours selecting a font that is "modern but not too aggressive."
3. **The Social Parasite Variable:** Each group is intentionally assigned one "Ghost Member"—an employee from a different department who is not required to contribute but has full voting rights on the final slide deck. This member is permitted to enter the group chat once every two weeks to ask, "Are we still on for Friday?"
4. **The Zero-Sum Reward Structure:** Upon successful completion of a project, the team is awarded a single, non-transferable $25 gift card to a regional seafood franchise. The group must then engage in a moderated, three-hour debate to determine who deserves the card, after which the card is shredded and the confetti is distributed equally among the six participants.

## Operational Context

This pivot follows the failure of Kinetix Group's "Hyper-Autonomy" pilot, which was shuttered after several employees finished their weekly quotas by Tuesday afternoon. Internal audits revealed that these employees were using their remaining time to engage in "dangerous levels of personal reflection" and "unmonitored hobbies," creating a productivity vacuum that threatened the company's culture of perpetual urgency.

## Internal Response

Early feedback is currently being processed by a 12-person Oversight Committee. The committee has spent the last three weeks arguing over whether the feedback survey should be a multiple-choice form or a series of open-ended prompts, resulting in a total standstill that the company has hailed as a "triumph of collaborative deliberation."

An internal FAQ clarifies that employees who feel they are doing 100% of the work are encouraged to "lean into the martyrdom," as this prepares them for a future leadership role where they will oversee six people doing 0% of the work.

Kinetix Group builds solutions that empower organizations to spend more time talking about work than actually performing it. Kinetix Group is headquartered in Chicago and operates 14 regional offices, each strategically located exactly 14 minutes too far to walk to the nearest pharmacy.
