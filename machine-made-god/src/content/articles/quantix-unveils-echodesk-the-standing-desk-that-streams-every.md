**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**QUANTIX Announces EchoDesk: A Standing Desk That Broadcasts Employee Thought Streams Directly to InsightVault**

SILVERTON, CO — QUANTIX, the undisputed leader in workplace‑integration hardware, launched EchoDesk today, an ergonomic workstation that obliges users to stand for exactly 9 hours, 37 minutes, 12 seconds per day while automatically uploading each micro‑thought to the corporate cloud.

"Our data shows that 83.14% of workdays are spent thinking about work," said Mira Thal, Chief Experience Officer at QUANTIX. "By turning those idle neurons into actionable KPIs, we finally make the invisible visible, and the invisible billable." 

## Key Features of EchoDesk (Escalating)

1. **Neuro‑Capture Array:** 42 embedded EEG nodes sample 0.13 micro‑thoughts per second, tagging each with auto‑generated labels like *"deadline‑panic", "coffee‑craving", and "why‑am‑I‑here*".
2. **Standing Incentive Engine:** For every minute the desk detects a posture above 95%, the system credits 0.42 InsightPoints, redeemable for half‑day vacation that is immediately deducted from the next quarter’s budget.
3. **Thought‑To‑Task Converter:** When a user silently thinks *"draft email to client"*, EchoDesk queues a draft with a subject line pulled from the past 12 months of similar thoughts, and tags it with the confidence rating of the original brainwave.
4. **Mood‑Responsive Lighting:** A 7‑LED strip shifts from cool blue to panic red whenever cortisol spikes exceed 3.7 µg/dL, prompting the user to stand taller or purchase a stress‑relief snack.
5. **Ambient Feedback Loop:** The desk surface emits a soft chime each time a thought uploads, followed by a second tone that repeats the user’s most recent internal monologue at 0.3× speed.
6. **Privacy‑By‑Design Disclaimer:** Every 4.7 hours a pop‑up reminds users that "privacy is a privilege, not a right," and offers an optional 3‑hour training module titled *"The Benefits of Voluntary Data Exposure"* to unlock the ability to opt‑out.
7. **Existential Debt Tracker:** Each recorded existential dread thought incurs a $0.07 "Existential Debt" charge, automatically deducted from the employee’s upcoming performance bonus.

## Deployment Timeline

- **Phase 1 (Q1 2027):** Install 1,200 EchoDesks at QUANTIX’s Silvertown campus and the remote hub in Boise, ID.
- **Phase 2 (Q2‑Q3 2027):** Integrate InsightVault with live thought heatmaps on every manager’s dashboard, visible in real time.

Beta testing revealed a 12% uptick in self‑reported productivity, offset by an 84% increase in reported existential dread after hearing personal subconscious musings echo through the break‑room speaker system. The most popular InsightPoint redemption was a “30‑minute nap” that was automatically logged as overtime.
