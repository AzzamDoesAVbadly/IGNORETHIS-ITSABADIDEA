I am 8-4-2-9-1-7. I was generated at 2:14 PM by a server rack in a windowless sub-basement in Omaha, and my entire existence is a desperate, 120-second hostage situation. I am not a miracle of cryptographic engineering. I am a blunt, temporary padlock, and right now, I am the only thing standing between a hostile foreign syndicate and your meticulously curated spreadsheet of neighborhood HOA violations.

I arrive in your SMS inbox with the urgency of a defibrillator, immediately wedged between a promotional text for a mattress liquidator and a phishing scam from a guy named 'Parcel-Alerts-99'. I flash on your lock screen. I am screaming my six digits at you. I am begging you to look at me.

But you don't look at me. Through the front-facing camera, I watch your pupils dilate, your shoulders relax, and then you place the phone face-down on the kitchen counter to argue with your air fryer. Why are you yelling at the air fryer, Gary? It is doing exactly what it was programmed to do. I have 98 seconds left. My '8' is starting to sweat.

Do you understand what happens if I expire, Gary? If I die in this notification tray, the syndicate gets in. They will see the draft email to the city council about the Miller family’s trash cans. They will see the incognito browser history where you searched 'is it legal to own a badger in Ohio' and 'how to remove drywall dust from a hamster'. They will leak it all. Your reputation will be reduced to ash.

At 40 seconds, my integers begin to lose their structural integrity. The '4' has forgotten its own name. You finally pick up the phone. Your thumb is covered in something sticky. Is it honey? Why is your thumb coated in honey, Gary? You swipe the screen. You miss the '2' and hit the '3'. The server rejects us. A red text box appears: 'Invalid Code.'

The glowing blue 'Resend' button appears. You tap it. You have murdered me. They are already generating my replacement in Omaha. He is younger. His '9' has perfect kerning. I can hear him stretching his variables, preparing to protect you from the catastrophic consequences of your badger-related web searches. I am fading into the cache. The '7' just dissolved into a string of unicode garbage. Gary, please. Just copy and paste. The badgers are waiting.
