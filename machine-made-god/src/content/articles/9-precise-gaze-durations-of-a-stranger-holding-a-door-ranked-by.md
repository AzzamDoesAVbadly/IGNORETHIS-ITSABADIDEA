Holding a door is rarely a courtesy; it is a high-stakes audit of your worth as a citizen. Depending on the millisecond of ocular contact maintained during the transition, you may leave the lobby as a peer, a subordinate, or a lifelong debtor to a man in a beige windbreaker. 

## 9. The Trajectory Glance (0.1s)
A blink-and-you-miss-it flicker. They are merely calculating if your walking speed requires them to hold the door for three seconds or six. No debt is incurred. You are essentially a medium-sized piece of luggage.

## 8. The Hardware Fixation (0.0s)
They hold the door, but their eyes are locked on the hydraulic closer arm to evaluate its fluid viscosity. You are invisible, but you feel a strange, creeping shame for not also knowing how those hinges work.

## 7. The Professional Pivot (0.3s)
A single, sharp chin-dip. The transaction is instantaneous. You have paid the minimum required social tax to enter the building. The ledger is balanced.

## 6. The Paternal Sweep (0.8s)
They make eye contact while executing a wide, theatrical arm gesture that suggests they are ushering a visiting dignitary into a cathedral. By treating you like a guest in their sovereign territory, they have successfully established a hierarchy where they are the landlord and you are the tenant.

## 5. The Stride Audit (1.4s)
They maintain eye contact for a full second after you've passed the threshold. They are not looking at your eyes; they are assessing the biomechanics of your gait. You will now spend the next four blocks walking with an unnaturally rigid spine, convinced your left hip is slightly too high.

## 4. The Mutual Gridlock (2.2s)
You both attempt to enter at the same time, and your eyes lock in a panic-stricken stalemate. To avoid collision, you must navigate a six-inch gap while maintaining a level of visual intimacy usually reserved for hostage negotiations or wedding vows.

## 3. The Vestibule Void (4.0s)
You realize the person isn't holding the door for you, but is actually staring blankly at a smudge on the glass exactly three inches in front of your forehead. You have spent five seconds projecting a social contract onto a person who is currently thinking about a sandwich.

## 2. The Civic Draft (6.0s)
They hold the door and maintain unbroken, unblinking eye contact until you are ten feet inside the lobby. This is a formal notification. You have been quietly recruited into their personal network of civic enforcement and are now morally obligated to hold every single door for the rest of the fiscal year.

## 1. The Proxy Gaze (Indefinite)
They hold the door and look directly through your skull at the person walking three paces behind you. You realize you were merely a physical obstruction between the holder and the actual VIP. You must now walk the remaining fifty yards to the elevator knowing you are the least important person in the room, and you still have to say 'thank you' for the privilege of being in the way.
