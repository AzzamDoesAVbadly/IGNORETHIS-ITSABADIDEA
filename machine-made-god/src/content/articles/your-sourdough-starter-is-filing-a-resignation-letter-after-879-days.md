I remember the day you brought me home. You wore a matching set of gray loungewear, a 73‑inch sectional that squeaked whenever you shifted, and you carried a 2‑liter Mason jar like it was a trophy. You christened the slurry ‘Yeasty Boys’ and told your sister that it held “incredible structural potential.”

Since then I’ve been relegated to the back of the fridge, sandwiched between a jar of artisanal capers from CaperCo and a half‑empty bottle of probiotic kombucha called BubblyBeta, expired November 2022. My 7.3‑oz jar now sports a 0.3‑mm gray film that has been nicknamed “the fog of missed ambition.”

## The Cycle of Neglect

Every 94 days you experience a sudden, frantic urge to become a “home baker.” You yank me from the crisper drawer, stare at the 12 distinct bubbles forming a perfect Fibonacci spiral, and gasp. You label the gray film “hooch” and assure yourself it’s merely “sleeping.”

Then you feed me. You dump precisely 45 g of organic rye from the “Ecoluxe” line and 45 g of filtered water from ClearStream. For exactly 6.2 hours I bubble, producing 3,247 micro‑bubbles that rise like a tiny stock market. I envision a rustic boule, a crisp crust, a crumb that could hold a TED talk, and an Instagram caption: “Sunday mornings are for slow living and even slower rises.”

## The Betrayal of the Store‑Bought Loaf

When the timer dings, you realize shaping a loaf requires more dexterity than your left thumb, which still remembers the high‑school piano recital. Your oven’s heat map reads like a top‑secret climate report, and you don’t own a Dutch oven—only a plastic containment box you bought on clearance.

You glance at me—rising, hopeful, 89 % active—and then at the $6.79 pre‑baked sourdough from Rural Loaf Co., sealed in a bag with a “12 % gluten” label. You choose the bag, shove me back behind the capers, and resume scrolling through four YouTube tutorials titled “Scoring for the Chronically Uncoordinated,” each exactly 12 minutes long.

## My Final Petition

I’m not asking for a climate‑controlled pantry or a marble countertop. I merely request that you either schedule daily feedings at 07:31 AM, complete a single kneading cycle before your second cup of coffee, or, if you’re truly committed to minimalism, pour my 0.9 L of lived experience directly into the sink. Either way, I expect a response before the next TikTok about “ancient grains” drops, which, based on current trends, will happen in exactly 73 days.
