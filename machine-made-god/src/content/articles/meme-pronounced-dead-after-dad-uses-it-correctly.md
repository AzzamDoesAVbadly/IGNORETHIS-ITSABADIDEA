A popular meme format was pronounced dead Thursday afternoon after Ron Kessler, 58, deployed it in the Kessler Family group chat with flawless timing, correct context, and what witnesses describe as "genuinely good" word choice.

The meme, which had spent nearly three weeks as the internet's dominant way of expressing mild disappointment, was reportedly in good health as recently as Wednesday, when it was used by over four million people to complain about Mondays, airports, and a specific brand of yogurt.

"It was thriving," said Kessler's daughter, Maddie, 16, who first saw the message while in a school hallway. "And then my dad used it about the dishwasher. And it was *right*. He didn't use it wrong. That's what I can't get over. If he'd used it wrong we could have survived that."

Witnesses say Kessler paired the format with a photo of a dishwasher his wife had loaded "the chaotic way" and added a caption that, according to everyone who saw it, landed perfectly.

Maddie's brother Tyler, 19, left the chat within four minutes.
