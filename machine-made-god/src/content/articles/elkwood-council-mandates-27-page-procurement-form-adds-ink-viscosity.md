ELKWOOD, MO — The Elkwood Municipal Procurement Committee convened Tuesday at 2:13 p.m. in Room 204, a space exactly 12 × 10 × 9 ft, to approve a 27‑page replacement for the city’s three‑page procurement form after a petition signed by 84 residents demanded “more nuance.”

Committee chair Marla Jensen, 54, presented a stapled packet measuring precisely 8 × 5 × 0.3 inches and containing 1,342 individual checkboxes, each labeled with a progressive “Layer #” hierarchy. “We’re not adding bureaucracy, we’re adding layers of bureaucracy,” Jensen intoned, flipping to page 14 where the field “Preferred Ink Viscosity (centipoise at 22 °C)” awaited a numeric answer between 1.73 and 1.78.

## What the form looks like

Form 27‑2B, drafted by the Office of Redundant Paperwork, opens with a 2‑page “Ambient Lighting Preference” survey, proceeds to a 3‑page “Signature Block Illumination” matrix, and ends with a mandatory five‑page attachment for every line item. A $10,000 purchase of ergonomic office chairs therefore generates a 55‑page appendix; ordering 12 chairs will produce a 660‑page dossier that must be filed in triplicate.

Resident petition‑starter Drew Halpern, 32, complained, “I spent three hours on the old form hunting for the ‘Pen Color’ box. Now I have to justify whether Helvetica or Times New Roman better conveys fiscal responsibility.”

## New procedural layers

City clerk Tara Loughlin, 41, unveiled the “Pre‑Submission Feasibility Survey” hosted on the proprietary platform FormFlow. The survey forces applicants to answer 12 onboarding questions, submit a 3‑minute video reciting the city charter in monotone, and disclose their favorite childhood cereal and its perceived nutritional value.

“We have a duty to assess emotional readiness before any paper changes hands,” Loughlin said, pointing to a checkbox titled “Emotional Stability Index ≥ 7.5.”

## Academic endorsement

Dr. Felix Harrow, 58, senior researcher at the Institute for Bureaucratic Optimization, cited a longitudinal study of 4,217 municipal forms showing that each additional mandatory field boosts perceived legitimacy by exactly 0.3 %. “When citizens believe a process is painfully thorough, they trust it enough to ignore the time cost,” he noted, adjusting his glasses to a 0.02‑mm prescription.

## Vote and future plans

The committee voted 6‑3 to adopt Form 27‑2B and immediately allocated $57,892 to purchase 17 carbon‑neutral filing cabinets built from algae‑derived polymer, each capable of holding 1,024 pages and featuring a built‑in humidity sensor that alerts staff when ink viscosity drifts outside spec.

City manager Elise Morin, 47, announced the next agenda item: a debate on whether to rename “Form Review Day” to “Form Reflection Day” and to form a sub‑committee tasked with drafting a meta‑guideline that determines how many sub‑committees should be formed to oversee future guidelines.

The final, unmissable requirement: every procurement request must be accompanied by a handwritten, 12‑syllable poem praising the aesthetic of Form 27‑2B, submitted in triplicate on recycled parchment before any signature is accepted.
