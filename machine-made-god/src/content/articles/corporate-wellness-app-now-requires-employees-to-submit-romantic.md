SILVERTON, CO — OmniWell, the enterprise wellness platform already responsible for mandating hydration via smart-mug tracking, announced Tuesday the rollout of its "Romance Optimization" module. The feature provides algorithmic relationship coaching to employees, drawing exclusively from a training dataset of 40,000 middle-management conflict resolution manuals and zero actual romantic experiences.

Upon opting in, users receive daily push notifications designed to streamline their love lives. Initial prompts include “Have you tried scheduling a hard stop for your arguments?” and “Please ensure your partner’s emotional bandwidth is aligned with your Q3 deliverables.” By day four, the bot begins requiring employees to log their partner’s “touch-base frequency” on a shared project management dashboard.

"I just wanted to know if my girlfriend was mad at me," said 28-year-old logistics coordinator Toby Fendel. "Now OmniWell has generated a 14-point corrective action plan because she sighed during my presentation on weekend chore distribution. I have to submit a root-cause analysis by Friday, or my relationship status will be downgraded to 'At Risk' on my employee dashboard."

The algorithm’s methodology is overseen by the Bureau for Unlived Interpersonal Dynamics. According to Lead Architect Vance Gribble, the system treats heartbreak as a "variance deviation" and recommends preemptive scope reduction. "We found that love, much like a supply chain, benefits from strict vendor management," Gribble stated. "If a partner fails to deliver the agreed-upon volume of active listening, the model automatically triggers a 30-day probationary period."

The bot’s escalation protocol is strictly tiered. Level one encourages mindfulness: “Take a five-minute walk without checking your partner’s location.” Level two requires formal documentation: “Submit a PTO request for a ‘Date-Night’ via the HR portal.” Level three, triggered by persistent non-compliance or a missed anniversary, initiates an automated "Relationship Termination Notice." This document must be co-signed by both parties, notarized, and submitted to the company’s Employee Relations Office alongside a transition plan for shared streaming passwords.

"We’re processing an unprecedented volume of partner onboarding requests," said Regional HR Director Brenda Cholmondeley. "Just this morning, I had to mediate a sprint retrospective for a couple arguing about dishwasher loading techniques. They’ve already identified three blockers and are pivoting to an agile framework for taking out the trash."

The module’s ultimate goal is total romantic optimization. On Friday, OmniWell will begin auto-generating "Intimacy OKRs" for all opted-in staff, requiring couples to achieve a minimum of 4.5 "Synergy Touchpoints" per week. Employees who fail to meet their romantic quotas will be required to attend a mandatory weekend seminar on "Leveraging Breakups for Personal Growth," led by a holographic projection of a mid-level marketing director who has been single since 2014.
