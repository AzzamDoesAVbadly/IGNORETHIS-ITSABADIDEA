OAK HAVEN, FL — The Oak Haven Homeowners Association convened a three‑person Special Task Force on Thursday, assigning each member a calibrated dandelion‑pollen detector and a copy of the HOA’s 2022 "Noise‑Induced Aggression" handbook.

The task force was activated after five formal complaints described a homeowner who allegedly uses his leaf blower not to clear debris but to stage a "performative landscaping" spectacle aimed at unsettling the cul‑de‑sac.

"It’s not about the leaves; it’s about the intent," said HOA Vice President Brenda St. Claire, 58, while gesturing to a 3‑by‑5‑foot poster that plotted wind‑direction vectors, leaf‑trajectory angles, and the exact seconds each leaf lingered over the Johnsons’ front porch.

## Evidence

Task‑force analysts have reviewed 14 hours of high‑definition EchoCam footage captured by six perimeter cameras, cataloguing 4,732 individual leaf fragments and three distinct blower‑engine tonal shifts.

The memorandum lists the most incriminating patterns:

- A precisely timed 14‑second pause occurring at 02:13 a.m., which the committee interprets as Greg waiting for a neighbor to peek out their bedroom curtain.
- The relocation of a single, damp maple leaf from the gutter to the exact center of Mrs. Patel’s welcome mat at 07:42 a.m., a move measured at 0.03 inches from the mat’s decorative border.
- A 4:00 a.m. “respiratory dominance” session in which the blower emitted a sustained 78 dB hum for 12 minutes, synchronously with the HOA’s own night‑shift security drone.

"I felt it in my marrow," said resident Arthur Pringle, 64, who reported that Greg repeatedly cycled the same six leaves around a clockwise spiral for twenty‑seven minutes, each rotation timed to the HOA’s quarterly budget release.

## Expert analysis

The HOA retained Dr. Lawrence Finch, Senior Fellow at the Institute for Residential Friction, whose specialty is "passive‑aggressive outdoor maintenance." Dr. Finch warned that the phenomenon represents a new class of suburban signaling he terms "Atmospheric Gaslighting."

"What we’re witnessing is a power tool‑driven soundscape of productivity that accomplishes nothing but asserts the operator’s leisure time and gasoline budget," Dr. Finch explained. "It’s the auditory equivalent of posting a vacation photo while never actually leaving the driveway."

## Current status

As of Friday evening, the Task Force issued a formal Warning of Intent to Greg, demanding a written "Blowing Schedule," a top‑ographic map of his intended leaf‑trajectory for the weekend, and a signed affidavit attesting that his blower does not contain any concealed recording devices.

Greg has not commented, though neighbors observed him polishing his blower’s nozzle with a custom‑woven microfiber glove while maintaining steady eye contact with the HOA board’s parked SUV. The HOA announced plans to convene a public hearing on Monday, during which all attendees will be required to wear earplugs calibrated to mute leaf‑blower frequencies below 80 dB.
